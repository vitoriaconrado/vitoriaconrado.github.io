01 / About

A biologist in
progress.

I’m Vitória, a biology student from southern Brazil interested in the places where living systems meet human systems.

I’m a first-year undergraduate at UFSC, a member of its AltProtein Project (connected to GFI Brasil), a data analysis intern, and a junior researcher investigating Brazil’s cultivated-meat regulatory scene.
